# Fallen castle / guard camp assets

Environment props are **original** meshes built in-engine for Line 7:

- Palisade: cylinder + cone logs with generated bark albedo
- Lean-tos: pole logs + double-sided cloth planes
- Crates / barrels: plank-textured boxes and hooped cylinders
- Tripod, sacks, torch posts, timber ramps, mud/grass ground
- Leather hide drape (generated orange-brown albedo)

Textures are generated at runtime in `camp_kit.gd` (no third-party game files, no Elden Ring assets).

Kenney CC0 kits were evaluated (Fantasy Town Kit) and **not used** — their pastel town pieces did not match the camp reference.
