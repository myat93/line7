# Fallen castle / guard camp assets

## Meshes
Original in-engine props in `camp_kit.gd`: tapered sharpened palisade logs, sagging cloth lean-tos, plank-framed crates, hooped barrels, lashed-pole tripod, iron-bowl torch posts, timber ramps, misty valley hills.

No Elden Ring / ripped game files. Cartoon town kits (Kenney Fantasy Town, Quaternius Fantasy RTS) were evaluated and **not used** — they do not read as a grim outpost.
